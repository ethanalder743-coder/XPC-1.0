XPC Bot Privacy Policy

Effective 27 August 2026

This policy explains what information XPC processes when you use the Discord bot.

Information processed

XPC may store Discord user, server, channel and role IDs; team and roster configuration; offers, transfers, budgets and results; saved-role records; ticket and application answers; moderation warnings and logs; invite attribution; and statistics or images users intentionally submit.

How information is used

Information is used to operate requested bot features, preserve server configuration, provide audit records, prevent abuse and troubleshoot the service.

Storage and retention

Operational data is stored in the bot's database on its hosting provider and retained while needed to provide the service or until removed by an authorised administrator.

Sharing

XPC does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by Discord and Railway as needed to run the service, or disclosed when legally required.

Security and choices

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Server owners or affected users may request access, correction or deletion through the official XPC Discord community or their server administrator.

Children and changes

Users must meet Discord's minimum age and any higher age required in their country. This policy may be updated when features or legal requirements change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the XPC operator through the official XPC Discord community.
